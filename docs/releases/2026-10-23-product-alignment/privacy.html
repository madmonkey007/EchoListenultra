Privacy and your data

Data and privacy · Updated October 23, 2026

Processing a public Reel

When you submit a public Instagram Reel link, our server retrieves the audio and sends it to Cloudflare Workers AI to generate text and timestamped subtitles. No Instagram login or personal transcription API key is required.

Only submit content you own or have permission to use. Leaving the page or stopping the wait may not stop processing that has already begun.

Results on this page

The submitted link and generated transcript are held in the current page and are no longer retained there after a refresh. Use Copy text or Download SRT before leaving if you want to keep the result. ReelTranscript does not provide a transcript history or cross-device sync.

Your theme preference is saved in this browser. You can change it in Settings or clear site storage using your browser settings.

Service providers

Instagram, Cloudflare and the hosting provider process requests needed to retrieve and transcribe the Reel. They may retain request information under their own policies. The website also loads fonts and interface resources from external services, which receive ordinary network information such as your IP address.

Usage and reliability

The website may send basic action names and the app version to its own usage endpoint. These event payloads do not include audio, transcript text, submitted links or email addresses. Hosting request logs and error diagnostics are separate from these events.

Contacting the maintainer

The contact page prepares an email draft. Nothing is sent until you send it through your email application. Your email address and any details you choose to include are used to respond to your message.

Audio and transcripts are not attached automatically. Review your draft and avoid including private content or credentials.
