About ReelTranscript

A personal listening workspace

ReelTranscript is an individually maintained English audio workspace. Bring your own audio, divide it into manageable sections and repeat the passages you want to understand.

You can adjust playback speed, follow a transcript when one is available, and save words for later review. Time-based sections work without a transcription key. Sentence and speaker sections need a successful transcript from your chosen provider.

Start without an account

Local listening needs no sign-up. Optional Google sign-in is for online allowance where configured. Your audio library, saved words and preferences live in the browser you use. There is no cloud backup or cross-device sync, so keep your original audio files safe.

Automatic transcription is optional and uses your own provider key. Read how your data is handled before enabling it.

Built around your practice

Try a short recording first, choose a section and listen again at your own pace. For a walkthrough, visit the listening guide.

This is a small, independently maintained project. If something breaks or gets in your way, send feedback. Replies may take time.
